    The road to the platinum

    Five stages, in order, built only from what the official descriptions say. Story first — the chapter and mission trophies unlock the map and the contacts — then side strands, then exploring and collecting, then activities and combat, and finally money, choices and clean-up. The platinum pops the moment the 54th trophy lands.

    If a story-mission trophy does not pop when the mission ends, replay that named mission: the description names the mission, and replaying it is the way back. Nothing in the official list marks a story trophy as missable.

    Missables — the 9 to watch

    These trophies all hinge on a single attempt, a single run, or a choice: fail the attempt and the run is over, so plan before you start. Everything else in the list can be picked up later — story missions are named in their descriptions, and counts (stunts, races, cards, areas) are cumulative.
